
# NBA Team Wiki - Agent Instructions

## Project Goal
Build a reliable knowledge base about NBA team management and analytics.

## Knowledge Organization
- index.md: Wiki homepage and navigation
- team-management.md: NBA team management
- salary-cap.md: Salary cap and luxury tax
- player-analytics.md: Basketball analytics metrics
- trades-and-draft.md: Trades and draft strategies
- team-building.md: Team building strategies

## Ingestion Rules
1. Read original sources before updating the Wiki.
2. Check whether related information already exists.
3. Extract important facts, definitions and explanations.
4. Record the original source and publication date when available.
5. Never invent statistics, rules or facts.
6. Mark uncertain or conflicting information clearly.

## Wiki Maintenance
1. Update the most relevant knowledge page.
2. Add links to related Wiki pages.
3. Check existing links after updating.
4. Avoid unnecessary duplication.
5. Update index.md when adding a new topic.

## Quality Check
- Verify factual accuracy against original sources.
- Check whether explanations are understandable to beginners.
- Check source references.
- Check internal links.
- Identify outdated NBA rules or statistics.

## Logging
Record every ingestion, update, query and quality check in LOG.md.
