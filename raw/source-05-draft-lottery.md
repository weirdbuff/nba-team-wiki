## 1. What Is the NBA Draft?
The NBA Draft is an annual event through which NBA teams select eligible players.

It allows teams to add young talent to their rosters and develop players for the future.

## 2. What Is the NBA Draft Lottery?
The NBA Draft Lottery determines the selection order for the first 16 picks of the NBA Draft under the new system beginning in 2027.

The lottery is designed to give eligible teams different chances of receiving early draft picks.

## 3. What Is the New 3-2-1 Lottery System?
The NBA approved a new Draft Lottery system in May 2026.

It will take effect beginning with the 2027 NBA Draft and remain in effect for the 2027, 2028 and 2029 drafts.

Key rules include:

- The lottery expands from 14 to 16 teams.
- Teams that miss both the playoffs and the Play-In Tournament generally receive three lottery balls.
- The three teams with the worst regular-season records lose one lottery ball each.
- The No. 9 and No. 10 Play-In seeds in each conference receive two lottery balls each.
- The losers of the No. 7 vs. No. 8 Play-In games receive one lottery ball each.

The lottery determines the first 16 selections.

## 4. How Are the Remaining Picks Determined?
- Picks 17-30 in the first round are determined by reverse order of regular-season records among the 14 teams outside the lottery.
- Picks 31-60 in the second round are determined by reverse order of regular-season records among all 30 teams.
- Draft picks may be affected by trades and forfeitures.

## 5. Why Is the Draft Important to Team Management?
The Draft is an important way for teams to acquire young players.

Teams may use draft picks to:
- Develop young players.
- Add talent to the roster.
- Build a team for the future.
- Trade draft picks to acquire established players.

## 6. Key Takeaways
1. The Draft allows teams to acquire eligible young players.
2. The Draft Lottery determines the order of the first 16 picks under the 2027 system.
3. The 3-2-1 system is designed to reduce incentives for teams to prioritize losing games.
4. Draft picks are valuable assets in team-building and trade decisions.
5. Teams must balance short-term competitiveness with long-term development.
