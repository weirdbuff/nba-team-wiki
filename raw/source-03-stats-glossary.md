## 1. What is the NBA Statistics Glossary?
The NBA Statistics Glossary explains the definitions and formulas of basketball statistics used on NBA Stats.

It includes traditional statistics, advanced statistics, shooting statistics and player tracking data.

## 2. Important Advanced Statistics

### TS% (True Shooting Percentage)
- Measures shooting efficiency while considering field goals, three-point shots and free throws.
- Formula: Points / [2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)]
- Use: Evaluate a player's overall scoring efficiency.

### USG% (Usage Percentage)
- Measures the percentage of team plays used by a player while on the floor.
- Use: Understand how much offensive responsibility a player takes.

### eFG% (Effective Field Goal Percentage)
- Adjusts field goal percentage to account for the additional value of three-point shots.
- Formula: (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA
- Use: Compare shooting efficiency while considering shot value.

### PACE
- Measures the number of possessions per 48 minutes for a team or player.
- Use: Understand a team's playing speed.

### PIE (Player Impact Estimate)
- Measures a player's overall statistical contribution compared with the total statistics in games they play.
- Use: Provide an overall view of a player's statistical contribution.

### AST% (Assist Percentage)
- Measures the percentage of teammate field goals a player assisted on while they were on the floor.
- Use: Understand a player's contribution to team playmaking.

## 3. Why Are These Statistics Important?
Traditional statistics such as points, rebounds and assists do not explain every aspect of a player's performance.

Advanced statistics provide additional ways to evaluate:
- Scoring efficiency
- Offensive responsibility
- Playmaking contribution
- Overall statistical impact
- Team playing pace

## 4. Key Takeaways
1. TS% evaluates shooting efficiency while considering free throws and three-pointers.
2. USG% measures a player's share of team plays used.
3. eFG% adjusts shooting percentage based on the value of three-point shots.
4. PACE helps describe the speed of a game.
5. PIE provides an overall measure of statistical contribution.
6. Different statistics should be considered together rather than used individually.
