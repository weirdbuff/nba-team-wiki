
# Source 01: NBA Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA)

## Source Information
- Publisher: National Basketball Players Association (NBPA)
- Document: 2023 NBA-NBPA Collective Bargaining Agreement
- URL: https://www.nbpa.com/cba
- Effective date: July 1, 2023
- Coverage: Through the 2029-30 NBA season

## 1. What is the CBA?
The Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) is an agreement between the NBA and the National Basketball Players Association.

It establishes the employment terms of NBA players and defines the rights and responsibilities of NBA teams, the league and the players' association.

## 2. Why is the CBA important to team management?
The CBA affects many important team management decisions, including:
- Player contracts and salaries
- Salary cap regulations
- Luxury tax
- Player transactions
- Team roster management

## 3. Relationship with Salary Cap
The salary cap system is governed by the CBA.

Teams must consider salary regulations when signing players, extending contracts and completing trades.

## 4. Key Takeaways
1. The CBA provides the basic rules for NBA team and player employment.
2. Team management decisions must comply with the CBA.
3. Salary cap rules influence how teams build their rosters.
4. The CBA is essential for understanding NBA team management.
