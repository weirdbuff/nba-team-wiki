- Note: This document explains the 2024-25 rules and figures. Financial thresholds change by season.

## 1. What is the Salary Cap?
The Salary Cap is the amount that determines how much a team can spend on player salaries under the NBA's salary system.

The NBA uses a soft salary cap, which means teams may exceed the cap under certain exceptions and conditions.

## 2. What is the Luxury Tax?
The Luxury Tax is a financial penalty imposed on teams whose payroll exceeds the tax threshold.

It is designed to discourage excessive spending and promote competitive balance.

## 3. What are the First Apron and Second Apron?
The First Apron and Second Apron are additional payroll thresholds above the Luxury Tax level.

Teams that exceed these thresholds face stricter restrictions on certain roster-building and player transaction mechanisms.

## 4. Why do these rules matter?
NBA teams must consider salary rules when:
- Signing free agents
- Extending player contracts
- Making trades
- Managing the overall roster payroll

A team may have talented players but still face difficulties improving its roster because of salary restrictions.

## 5. Key Takeaways
1. The NBA uses a soft salary cap rather than a strict hard cap for all teams.
2. The Luxury Tax creates additional financial costs for high-spending teams.
3. The First Apron and Second Apron impose additional restrictions.
4. Salary rules influence how teams construct and improve their rosters.
5. Annual salary thresholds must be checked for the relevant season.
