# Source 04: NBA Statistics FAQ

## Source Information
- Publisher: NBA
- Document: NBA Stats Frequently Asked Questions
- URL: https://www.nba.com/stats/help/faq
- Topic: Advanced statistics and player tracking
- Source type: Official NBA FAQ

## 1. What Are Advanced Statistics?
Advanced statistics use objective analysis to provide a deeper understanding of basketball performance.

They help evaluate players and teams beyond traditional box score statistics.

## 2. What Is Player Tracking?
Player Tracking uses cameras installed in NBA arenas and tracking software to record the movements of players and the basketball.

This technology provides additional information about player movement and on-court activity.

## 3. Why Are the Four Factors Important?
The NBA FAQ identifies four important statistics related to winning:

- Effective Field Goal Percentage (eFG%)
- Turnover Ratio
- Offensive Rebound Percentage (OREB%)
- Free Throw Attempt Rate (FTA Rate)

These statistics help evaluate different aspects of team performance.

## 4. Why Should We Consider Pace?
Teams play at different speeds.

A faster team may have more possessions and therefore more opportunities to score.

Because of this, points per game alone may not provide a fair comparison of offensive performance.

Per-possession statistics can provide a more meaningful comparison.

## 5. What Is PIE?
Player Impact Estimate (PIE) measures a player's or team's statistical contribution relative to the total statistics in games they participate in.

It considers traditional statistics such as points, rebounds, assists and turnovers.

## 6. How Often Are Statistics Updated?
According to the NBA FAQ:
- Basic statistics are updated in real time during games.
- Advanced statistics are updated approximately 10–15 minutes after a game finishes.

## 7. Key Takeaways
1. Advanced statistics provide deeper insights than traditional box scores.
2. Player Tracking provides information about player and ball movement.
3. The Four Factors help evaluate important aspects of team performance.
4. Pace should be considered when comparing teams with different playing speeds.
5. Multiple statistics should be used together to evaluate team performance.
