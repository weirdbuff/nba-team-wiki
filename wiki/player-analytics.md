# NBA Player Analytics

## 1. Introduction

Player analytics uses statistical data to evaluate basketball players' performance and contributions.

Traditional statistics such as points, rebounds and assists provide basic information, while advanced statistics offer deeper insights into player efficiency and playing styles.

## 2. True Shooting Percentage (TS%)

TS% measures a player's shooting efficiency while considering field goals, three-point shots and free throws.

Formula:

TS% = Points / [2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)]

A higher TS% generally indicates better scoring efficiency.

## 3. Usage Percentage (USG%)

USG% measures the percentage of team plays used by a player while they are on the floor.

It helps explain how much offensive responsibility a player takes.

A high USG% does not necessarily mean a player is more efficient. It should be considered together with other statistics.

## 4. Effective Field Goal Percentage (eFG%)

eFG% adjusts field goal percentage to account for the additional value of three-point shots.

Formula:

eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA

It provides a better comparison of shooting efficiency than ordinary field goal percentage alone.

## 5. Player Impact Estimate (PIE)

PIE measures a player's statistical contribution relative to the total statistics in games they participate in.

It considers statistics such as:
- Points
- Rebounds
- Assists
- Steals
- Blocks
- Turnovers

PIE provides an overall statistical perspective, but it does not fully represent every aspect of a player's value.

## 6. Pace

Pace measures the number of possessions per 48 minutes for a team or player.

It helps explain how quickly a team plays.

Teams with different playing speeds may have different scoring opportunities, so points per game alone may not provide a fair comparison.

## 7. How Teams Use Player Analytics

NBA teams can use advanced statistics to:

- Evaluate player scoring efficiency.
- Compare offensive responsibilities.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses.
- Support player acquisition decisions.
- Analyze team performance.

However, statistics should be interpreted together with game context, player roles and team strategy.

## 8. Key Takeaways

1. TS% measures overall shooting efficiency.
2. USG% measures offensive responsibility.
3. eFG% accounts for the additional value of three-point shots.
4. PIE provides an overall statistical contribution measure.
5. Pace helps explain differences in playing speed.
6. No single statistic can fully evaluate a basketball player.
