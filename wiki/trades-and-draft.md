# NBA Trades and Draft

## 1. Introduction

Trades and the NBA Draft are two important ways for teams to acquire players and improve their rosters.

Teams must evaluate both short-term benefits and long-term consequences when making these decisions.

## 2. Player Trades

A trade occurs when two or more NBA teams exchange players, draft picks or other permitted assets.

Teams make trades for different reasons:

- Improve their starting lineup.
- Add depth to the roster.
- Reduce salary commitments.
- Acquire future draft picks.
- Change the team's competitive direction.

## 3. Draft Picks as Assets

Draft picks are valuable assets in NBA team management.

Teams may use draft picks to:
- Select young players.
- Develop future talent.
- Trade for established players.
- Build long-term roster flexibility.

A team may choose to keep its draft picks for future development or exchange them to improve its current roster.

## 4. NBA Draft

The NBA Draft is an annual event in which teams select eligible players.

It provides teams with an opportunity to acquire young talent and strengthen their rosters.

The Draft consists of two rounds, with 30 selections in each round before accounting for forfeitures or other adjustments.

## 5. NBA Draft Lottery

The Draft Lottery determines the selection order of lottery teams.

Under the new system approved in 2026, the 3-2-1 Lottery system will begin with the 2027 NBA Draft.

Its purpose includes reducing incentives for teams to deliberately lose games to improve their draft position.

The new system expands lottery participation to 16 teams and changes how lottery chances are allocated.

## 6. Trade Strategy: Buyers and Sellers

NBA teams may take different approaches depending on their competitive position.

### Buyers
- Usually focus on immediate success.
- May trade draft picks for established players.
- Aim to improve their chances of competing for a championship.

### Sellers
- May trade experienced players for future assets.
- Often prioritize young players and draft picks.
- Focus on long-term development.

## 7. Risks of Trades and Draft Decisions

Trades and draft decisions involve uncertainty.

Important considerations include:

- Player performance may not meet expectations.
- Young players require time to develop.
- Draft selections may not become successful NBA players.
- Trading future picks may reduce long-term flexibility.
- Salary rules may restrict certain transactions.

## 8. Key Takeaways

1. Trades allow teams to exchange players and other permitted assets.
2. Draft picks are important resources for team building.
3. The NBA Draft provides opportunities to acquire young talent.
4. The Draft Lottery determines the selection order of lottery teams.
5. Teams must balance immediate competitiveness with long-term development.
6. Trade decisions should consider player performance, financial restrictions and future assets.
